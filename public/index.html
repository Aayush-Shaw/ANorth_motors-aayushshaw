Canadian Auto Loan Calculator

The AutoNorth Motors auto loan calculator estimates your car loan payment. Enter a vehicle price, interest rate (APR), and loan term in months, and it returns the monthly, bi-weekly, and weekly payment plus the total interest paid over the life of the loan. A second mode works in reverse: enter a target monthly payment and it returns the maximum vehicle price you can afford. It also accounts for a down payment, a trade-in value, and any loan still owed on the trade-in.

How the monthly payment is calculated

Payments use the standard amortization formula. In words: the amount financed is multiplied by the periodic interest rate, then scaled by a factor that spreads the interest evenly across every payment so the loan reaches a zero balance on the final payment.

  • Amount financed = vehicle price − trade-in value + balance owed on the trade-in − down payment + sales tax.
  • Periodic rate = annual interest rate (APR) ÷ number of payments per year (12 monthly, 26 bi-weekly, 52 weekly).
  • Number of payments = loan term in years × payments per year.
  • Payment = amountFinanced × periodicRate × (1 + periodicRate)^n ÷ ((1 + periodicRate)^n − 1), where n is the number of payments. At 0% interest the payment is simply the amount financed ÷ n.

How sales tax is applied

Sales tax is applied to the vehicle price minus the trade-in value, following the common Canadian rule that tax is charged on the net amount after a trade-in — not on the full sticker price. The calculator uses a single adjustable tax-rate field (0–15%) so it works in any province or territory. The default is 5% (Alberta GST; Alberta has no PST). Buyers in other provinces should set the field to their combined provincial rate — for example British Columbia (GST + PST), Ontario (HST), or Quebec (GST + QST).

Worked example

A $30,000 vehicle financed over 72 months at 7% APR in Alberta (5% GST), with no trade-in and no down payment:

  • Sales tax: 5% of $30,000 = $1,500
  • Amount financed: $31,500
  • Monthly payment: about $537
  • Total interest over 72 months: about $7,167
  • Total paid: about $38,667

Figures are estimates for planning only and are not an offer of credit or a guaranteed rate. Your actual rate and payment depend on lender approval.
